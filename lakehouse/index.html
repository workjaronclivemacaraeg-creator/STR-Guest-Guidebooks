Getting here

Lakehouse is on Guist Creek Lake near Shelbyville and I-64. Your exact address and arrival directions are supplied with your confirmed reservation. Follow the host's directions and use extra care near the water after dark.

Parking

Free parking is available on the property. Keep the driveway and neighboring access points clear.

The Home

A quick reference for the amenities and important house information.

Kitchen & GatheringEnjoy a bright open-plan kitchen, living, and dining area with full-size appliances and plenty of space to reconnect.
Sleeping ArrangementsThe primary bedroom has a queen bed, generous closet space, and a full bathroom. The guest room/office includes a twin convertible couch for a third guest.
Deck & Lake ViewsRelax on the covered deck or follow the stairs toward the shaded lakeside sitting area. Bring your own kayaks if you plan to paddle.
Work & ComfortWi-Fi, a dedicated workspace, games, grilling space, and a steam shower make the home comfortable for leisure or working stays.

House Rules

  • Please respect the home and surrounding property.
  • Keep noise at a considerate level, especially during quiet hours.
  • Use extra care around the lake, outdoor stairs, deck, grill, and waterfront areas.
  • Keep the bathroom door closed and use the exhaust fan when operating the steam shower.
  • The listing currently notes that the bourbon fire pit is out of service; confirm availability with the host before planning to use it.
  • Report damage or maintenance issues as soon as possible.
