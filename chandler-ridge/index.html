Everything you need to get settled when you arrive.

Getting here

The retreat is in the countryside near Bagdad, Kentucky. Your exact address and arrival directions are supplied with your confirmed reservation. Because the property is private and rural, follow the host's directions and download them before leaving reliable cell service.

Parking

Free parking is available on the property. Keep travel lanes and cabin access clear so everyone in your group can arrive and depart safely.

The Home

A quick reference for the amenities and important house information.

Kitchen & GatheringPrepare group meals in the full kitchen, then gather throughout the 3,250-square-foot log cabin. The home is designed to feel comfortable for both small and large groups.
Sleeping ArrangementsBedroom 1 has a king, double, and single bed; Bedroom 2 has three doubles and three singles; Bedroom 3 has two doubles; and Bedroom 4 has a queen and sofa bed.
Game Room & Home TheaterThe basement game room includes a pool table, arcade machine, and home theater—an easy option for rainy days and relaxed movie nights.
Pond, Trails & Fire PitExplore the private acreage, walk the trails, enjoy lawn games, gather at the fire pit, or unwind on the deck overlooking the pond.

House Rules

  • Please respect the home and surrounding property.
  • Keep noise at a considerate level, especially during quiet hours.
  • Use the pond, trails, fire pit, and game-room equipment responsibly.
  • Pets are welcome according to the terms of your confirmed reservation.
  • Report damage or maintenance issues as soon as possible.
