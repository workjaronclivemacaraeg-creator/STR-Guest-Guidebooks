Arrival & Check-In

Everything you need to get settled when you arrive.

Getting here

The farmhouse is in Lawrenceburg, about five to six blocks from Historic Main Street. Your exact address and arrival details are supplied with your confirmed reservation. Follow the host's directions and allow extra time if you have a scheduled distillery or winery tour.

Parking

Free parking is available in the private driveway. Please keep the sidewalk and neighboring access points clear.

The Home

A quick reference for the amenities and important house information.

Kitchen & GatheringPrepare meals in the fully equipped kitchen, then relax together in the comfortable living room. The restored farmhouse gives groups room to reconnect after a day exploring.
Sleeping ArrangementsThe home has four bedrooms. The first includes a queen bed and sofa bed; each of the other three has a queen bed. The living-room couch provides another flexible place to rest.
Porches, Backyard & Fire PitEnjoy the covered front and back porches, a spacious private backyard, and evenings around the outdoor fire pit.
Work & LaundryWi-Fi, a dedicated workspace, and a stackable washer and dryer make longer stays and working trips more comfortable.

House Rules

  • Please respect the home and surrounding property.
  • Keep noise at a considerate level, especially during quiet hours.
  • Use the fire pit responsibly and follow any posted safety guidance.
  • Report damage or maintenance issues as soon as possible.
