Getting here

Moonshine Ridge is in the private Cabins at Birch Hollow community near Stanton. Your exact address is supplied with your confirmed reservation. Mountain roads can be narrow and winding, so arrive before dark when possible and follow the host's arrival directions rather than relying only on GPS.

Parking

Free driveway parking is available on the property. Please keep access areas clear and use extra care when turning around on the mountain drive.

Explore the Area

Red River Gorge is celebrated for sandstone cliffs, natural arches, hiking, climbing, paddling, and scenic drives through Daniel Boone National Forest.

Natural Bridge State Resort Park

Walk beneath or hike to the top of Kentucky's iconic sandstone arch. The park also offers trails, overlooks, and a seasonal skylift.

Plan your visit
Red River Gorge Geological Area

Explore natural arches, dramatic cliffs, forest trails, and the scenic loop through Nada Tunnel in Daniel Boone National Forest.

Forest Service information
The Gorge Underground

Take a guided kayak or boat tour through a flooded limestone mine—an unusual adventure that works well in any weather.

View tours
More Local Adventures

Try ziplining, guided climbing, Hollerwood off-road trails, the Kentucky Reptile Zoo, Gladie Visitor Center, or a paddle on the Red River.
